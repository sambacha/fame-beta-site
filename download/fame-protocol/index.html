FAME puts every Solana token launch behind on-chain guarantees: liquidity locked at genesis, emissions capped in program constants, and one public price curve for everyone — the safety rules are in the ledger, not the marketing.

FAME is Solana fair-launch infrastructure for treasury and fund participation: program-enforced liquidity ratios, hard emission caps, deterministic vesting windows and a public audit trail — diligence you can re-derive from the chain.

The pattern

Fair launches fail the same three ways. So FAME removes the three doors.

Problem 01

The queue is rigged

Presales, insider allocations and sniper bots mean retail buys the top of the curve — and funds can't get size without private deals.

Problem 02

Liquidity evaporates

Teams pull the pool at the first red candle. An audit report is a snapshot of code, not a constraint on behaviour.

Problem 03

Emissions are a black box

Hidden unlocks and live mint authorities dilute holders on schedules nobody published.

Solution 01

One public curve

Every participant — human, treasury, or the team — buys from the same curve at the same price. Anti-sniper windows are enforced in-protocol.

Solution 02

Liquidity you can verify

Locked at genesis, ratio published every slot, circuit breaker armed below the floor. Exits are possible exactly when they're promised.

Solution 03

Emissions on a public schedule

The supply cap is a program constant and mint authority is burned. Every emission event is a readable transaction.

Who it's for

Two audiences. One rulebook.

01 / Retail

Enter at genesis

The same curve, the same price, the same slot as everyone else. Participation is the product — not access to a whitelist.

02 / Retail

Exit without rugs

Locked liquidity and a published ratio mean the exit you see is the exit you get. If the ratio breaks the floor, new deposits halt.

03 / Retail

No homework required

You don't need to read Rust. The invariants are checked continuously and the failures are loud, public and immediate.

01 / Institutional

Treasury participation

Deterministic launch windows sized for funds: no OTC negotiation, no side letters, no information asymmetry to document.

02 / Institutional

Exportable risk reporting

Liquidity-ratio history, emission schedules and upgrade timelocks are queryable — pipe them straight into your risk stack.

03 / Institutional

Custody-native

Program-derived vaults with no admin keys. Allocation and vesting settle on-chain, so custodians can verify without trusting a signer.
